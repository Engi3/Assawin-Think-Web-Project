Measurement & Control · Unit 8

Preventive Maintenance & Safety Inspection

The final unit ties every sensor from Units 1–7 into one habit: check it on a schedule, and never work on it live. Build a check sheet, then walk the Lockout–Tagout sequence step by step.

    Check items off as if you were walking the floor right now. Preventive maintenance beats breakdown maintenance because a five-minute check today avoids an unplanned stop next week.
