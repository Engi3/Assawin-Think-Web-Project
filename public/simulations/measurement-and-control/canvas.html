I/O Wiring Sketchpad

Before wiring a real PLC panel, sketch it here: sensors and field actuators on the outside, PLC input/output terminals in the middle. Use this to plan terminal assignments for the case studies in Units 1–5 before touching real hardware.

Click a terminal type, then click empty space to place it. Switch to Wire and click two terminals in turn to connect them (e.g. Sensor → PLC Input, PLC Output → Actuator). Double-click a terminal to delete it. Auto-saves in this browser.
