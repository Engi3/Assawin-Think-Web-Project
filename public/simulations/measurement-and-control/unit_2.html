Speed & Position Sensors

Encoders turn rotation into pulses; proximity sensors detect a target without touching it. Both feed a PLC's digital input or High-Speed Counter module — sweep the controls below to see how.

Incremental mode: count A/B pulses over time, divide by PPR, to get speed — the phase of A vs B gives direction. Speed = (pulses/sec ÷ PPR) × 60.

The sensor switches ON once the target is closer than its rated sensing distance Sn. Inductive sensors only trigger on metal targets; capacitive sensors trigger on almost any material (including liquids and plastics).
