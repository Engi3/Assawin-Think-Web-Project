Logic Circuit Sketchpad

Stamp gates onto the grid, then wire them together to sketch out a circuit idea before building it in the graded labs (Logic Gate Explorer, Combinational Logic, K-Map). This is a free scratchpad — it does not simulate signal values, it's for planning gate layout and connections.

Click a gate, then click empty space to place it. Switch to Wire and click two gates in turn to connect them. Double-click a gate to delete it. Your layout auto-saves in this browser.
