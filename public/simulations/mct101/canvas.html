Sensor & Actuator Wiring Sketchpad

Mechatronic systems are built from a signal loop: a sensor measures something in the world, a controller decides what to do, and an actuator acts on it, powered by a supply. Stamp these four block types onto the canvas and wire them in the order a real feedback loop would flow.

Click a block type, then click empty space to place it. Switch to Wire and click two blocks in turn to connect them in signal order (e.g. Sensor → Controller → Actuator). Double-click a block to delete it. Auto-saves in this browser.
